PROJECT: RUN BEYOND LIMITS 2026 (Marathon Website)
WEBSITE LINK: https://marathon-website-five.vercel.app/

WHAT IS THIS WEBSITE?
This is a website to register for the "Run Beyond Limits 2026" Marathon. 
- Runners can register and download their certificates.
- Event Organizers (Admins) can track all registrations and payments.

--------------------------------------------------

PART 1: FOR RUNNERS (How to Register)

Step 1: Go to the website link.
Step 2: Click on "Register Now".
Step 3: Fill your details (Name, Phone number, Email).
Step 4: Choose your City (Chennai, Bengaluru, Salem).
Step 5: Choose your Run Category (5K, 10K, or 21K) and T-Shirt size.
Step 6: Pay the fee.

After Payment:
Once paid, you will get an Email with your BIB Number and a PDF Certificate!

How to Download Your Certificate Again:
Just click "Download Certificate" on the website and enter your email or phone number.

--------------------------------------------------

PART 2: FOR ADMINS (How to Manage the Event)

Step 1: Go to the Admin Page: https://marathon-website-five.vercel.app/admin
Step 2: Login using:
        Username: admin
        Password: password123
Step 3: Inside the dashboard, you can see how many people registered and how much money was collected.
Step 4: Go to "Participants" to see the list of runners. 
Step 5: You can Approve their payments here. Once you click "Approve", the runner automatically gets their BIB Number and Email Certificate!
Step 6: You can also download all participants' data as an Excel file.
